Privacy Policy for Calm Zen Sound

At Calm Zen Sound, we care about your privacy. This page explains how we collect and use information.

Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads on our website. Google uses cookies (like the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other places on the internet.

You can choose to opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network privacy policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal secrets. We only see standard web logs like your browser type, device type, and the time you spend on our website to make your experience better.
